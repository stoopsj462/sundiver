Sundiver

A one-tap solar orbit arcade game for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy Policy Support

Tap to dive between orbits, dodge the solar flares, and grab every ember before the sun pulls you in. Unlock comet colors by beating your high score, and spend collected embers in the shop on new comet trails. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking — it works fully offline.
